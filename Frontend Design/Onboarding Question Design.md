## Onboarding Question Design Rationale

The onboarding questions are designed with two key goals:
1. Enhance user experience – help new users quickly understand what the platform offers.
2. Collect meaningful data – capture user preferences and interests to enable personalised recommendations and efficient filtering later on.
### 1. Focus on Purpose
The first question identifies the **user’s primary motivation** (e.g., explore companies, find suppliers, research industry trends). This ensures the system understands _why_ the user is here, aligning features with their goals.
### 2. Capture Sector Interests
As the ICN dataset is structured by **industry sectors and items**, asking users to select sectors of interest allows the platform to filter results more effectively. This reduces information overload and gives users a tailored starting point.
### 3. Company Attributes & Capabilities
Since companies can be classified as manufacturers, service providers, raw material suppliers, etc. , asking which types of companies users want to see makes the browsing experience more relevant. This also aligns directly with the filtering functions specified in requirements
### 4. Diversity & Certifications
Inclusion markers such as **female-owned, First Nations-owned, and certified businesses** are important both socially and for procurement policies. Including these as optional onboarding questions supports users who value diversity and compliance.
### 5. Personalisation & Flexibility
The final question (“Would you like tailored recommendations?”) respects user autonomy. Some users prefer **customised suggestions**, while others prefer to explore independently. Providing both options ensures inclusivity.
### 6. UX Principles
- One question per screen with a progress bar to maintain engagement.
- Skip option on optional questions to reduce drop-off.
- 3–5 core questions only, to balance data collection with smooth onboarding.
