> A **user flow** is the path a user takes through a website or application to complete a specific task. However, it can also be thought of as a visual narrative of a user's interaction with a product to achieve a goal. It maps every possible step, decision, and interaction from a designated entry point to a successful final action, serving as the essential blueprint for the user experience.
> 
> The primary purpose of a user flow diagram is to enable teams to analyse and optimise this path, ensuring it is as intuitive, efficient, and frictionless as possible.
>
