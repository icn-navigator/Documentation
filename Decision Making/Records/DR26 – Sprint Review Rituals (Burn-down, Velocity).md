## Background
During Sprint 1 feedback, the tutor suggested that sprint reviews would benefit from **visual artefacts** such as burn-down charts and velocity tracking. This helps the team measure progress, adjust planning, and demonstrate agile practice maturity.

## Relevant Data
- Current sprint reviews focus on demos and discussions but lack quantitative metrics.  
- GitHub Projects can generate basic charts or be integrated with external tools.  
- Tracking velocity helps anticipate workload capacity for future sprints.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: No metrics, discussion only**  
- Pro: Minimal effort.  
- Con: Harder to measure progress; weak agile evidence.  

**Option 2: Burn-down chart only**  
- Pro: Simple progress tracking.  
- Con: No insight into team velocity or workload capacity.  

**Option 3: Burn-down + velocity metrics (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Strong evidence of agile practice, better planning insights.  
- Con: Requires additional setup and consistent task tracking.  

## Action Items
- Configure GitHub Projects or external tool to generate burn-down charts.  
- Record story points or task counts to measure velocity.  
- Include these artefacts in sprint review presentations.  

## Outcome
The team decided to **add burn-down charts and velocity metrics to sprint reviews**.  
This provides stronger evidence of agile practice and supports better planning decisions.
