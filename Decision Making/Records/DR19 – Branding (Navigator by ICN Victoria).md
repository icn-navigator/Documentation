## Background
The client requested that the product name **“Navigator by ICN Victoria”** (or “ICN Victoria Navigator”) be clearly displayed within the application. This ensures consistent branding across communications, presentations, and the product interface.

## Relevant Data
- Client documents explicitly noted the importance of consistent product naming.  
- Branding consistency builds trust and recognition across industry and government users.  
- Inconsistent naming could cause confusion during demos and deployment.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: No explicit branding**  
- Pro: Cleaner UI, less text.  
- Con: Fails to meet client expectations and reduces brand recognition.  

**Option 2: Use alternative or abbreviated naming**  
- Pro: Potentially shorter and more flexible.  
- Con: Risks inconsistency with official brand identity.  

**Option 3: Use official naming “Navigator by ICN Victoria” (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Consistent, professional, aligned with client requirements.  
- Con: Slightly longer text in some UI placements.  

## Action Items
- Display branding clearly on landing page and navigation bar.  
- Ensure branding is used consistently in documentation and reports.  
- Verify consistency in deployment builds for client review.  

## Outcome
The team decided to **use the official product name “Navigator by ICN Victoria”** across all interfaces and documentation.  
This ensures professional branding and alignment with client requirements.
