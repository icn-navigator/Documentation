## Background
The client clarified that the Navigator platform must serve a **broad range of users**. It should not be limited to company representatives. Instead, the target audience includes government project owners, industry professionals, and the general public.

## Relevant Data
- Client Q&A confirmed that access is not restricted to companies.  
- ICN Victoria’s mission is to improve visibility across industries and connect suppliers with project owners.  
- Wider access promotes adoption and aligns with procurement policies.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: Company-only users**  
- Pro: Focused audience.  
- Con: Too restrictive, misses ICN Victoria’s wider goals.  

**Option 2: Industry + Government only**  
- Pro: Relevant to procurement use cases.  
- Con: Excludes general public, reduces transparency.  

**Option 3: Open access to industry, government, and general public (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Maximises visibility, aligns with client mission.  
- Con: Requires careful handling of sensitive data.  

## Action Items
- Design onboarding flows suitable for diverse users.  
- Ensure terminology and UI are accessible to non-specialists.  
- Clearly indicate ICN Victoria as the data owner to maintain trust.  

## Outcome
The team confirmed that **Navigator is accessible to the general public**, not just companies. The **target users** are:  
- Industry professionals  
- Government project owners  
- General public  
This decision aligns with ICN Victoria’s goals of transparency and broad industry engagement.
