## Background
The client clarified that any **communication features** in the Navigator app should not enable company-to-company chatting. Instead, they should connect users directly to ICN Victoria’s Customer Services team for enquiries, feedback, or reach-out requests.

## Relevant Data
- Client documents specify no company-to-company messaging.  
- Optional feature: enquiries should route to ICN Victoria (e.g., Customer Services email).  
- Not considered part of MVP, but valuable as a “nice to have”.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: Enable company-to-company chat**  
- Pro: Encourages networking.  
- Con: Conflicts with client requirements; high moderation burden.  

**Option 2: No communication features at all**  
- Pro: Simplifies MVP.  
- Con: Limits user ability to seek help or provide feedback.  

**Option 3: Communication only with ICN Victoria (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Meets client requirements, provides support channel.  
- Con: Limited scope compared to peer-to-peer communication.  

## Action Items
- Route all feedback/enquiry forms to ICN Victoria’s Customer Services.  
- Provide optional contact button on company profiles linking to ICN Victoria.  
- Document that this is an **optional** feature and not in MVP scope.  

## Outcome
The team decided that **any communication features will connect only to ICN Victoria**, not companies.  
This ensures compliance with client requirements while still offering a support channel.
