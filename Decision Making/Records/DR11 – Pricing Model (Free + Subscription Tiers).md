## Background
The client requested that the Navigator product remain accessible to the general public with a **free basic tier**. Additional functionality and detailed information can be placed under **up to three subscription tiers**. This ensures inclusivity while also providing options for monetisation and scalability.

## Relevant Data
- Free tier must always provide basic product usage.  
- Subscription tiers can unlock advanced data (e.g., revenue, size, certifications).  
- A maximum of three tiers (Basic, Plus, Premium) was agreed as manageable.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: Free only (no subscriptions)**  
- Pro: Maximum accessibility.  
- Con: No direct revenue model, limits sustainability.  

**Option 2: Free + 1 paid tier**  
- Pro: Simple model.  
- Con: May not capture full range of customer needs.  

**Option 3: Free + up to 3 subscription tiers (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Flexible, supports scaling with different client needs.  
- Con: Slightly more complexity in managing tiered features.  

## Action Items
- Define features allocated to each tier (Basic, Plus, Premium).  
- Ensure free tier remains fully functional and fair.  
- Document modular feature toggling to allow adjustments.  

## Outcome
The team decided on a **tiered pricing model**:  
- Free basic access for all users.  
- Up to 3 subscription tiers for advanced data and features.  
This balances **fair access** with a **sustainable revenue stream**.
