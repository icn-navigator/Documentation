## Background
The client encouraged the team to **share architecture flow diagrams and documentation** during development. These artefacts help ICN Victoria understand system design decisions and ensure alignment with enterprise standards.

## Relevant Data
- Sprint feedback highlighted the need for transparency in system design.  
- Architecture artefacts also support internal communication and onboarding of new developers.  
- The 4+1 model was suggested as a reference for structuring diagrams.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: Keep architecture docs internal only**  
- Pro: Less work in formatting for client.  
- Con: Reduces transparency; client cannot validate decisions.  

**Option 2: Share architecture diagrams and documentation with ICN Victoria (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Builds trust, supports client feedback, aligns with enterprise practices.  
- Con: Requires extra effort to prepare and maintain.  

## Action Items
- Create and maintain system architecture diagrams (component, deployment, data flow).  
- Link diagrams to decision log entries for traceability.  
- Share updated documentation via client’s shared folder before reviews.  

## Outcome
The team decided to **share architecture flow diagrams and documentation with ICN Victoria** on an ongoing basis.  
This ensures alignment with client expectations and improves communication and transparency.
