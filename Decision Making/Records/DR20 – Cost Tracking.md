## Background
The client suggested that the team track deployment and hosting costs, including licensing fees and scalability scenarios. However, the project is scoped as a small prototype/MVP, and most tools being used are **open-source or free**, meaning there is little cost impact to evaluate.

## Relevant Data
- Hosting is planned through lightweight services (Render free tier, PostgreSQL, MapBox student credits).  
- Frontend and backend frameworks (React, Bun, TypeScript) are open-source.  
- Tools like GitHub, Obsidian, and Font Awesome (basic icons) are free to use.  
- Tracking costs would add overhead without meaningful insight for this project.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: Track deployment and hosting costs in detail**  
- Pro: Provides financial scenarios for scalability.  
- Con: Out of scope for a small MVP; adds unnecessary work.  

**Option 2: Track only baseline deployment costs**  
- Pro: Easier than full cost modelling.  
- Con: Still requires updates with minimal benefit.  

**Option 3: No cost tracking (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Most tools are free/open-source; keeps scope lean and focused.  
- Con: Client will not have formal cost estimates.  

## Action Items
- Document decision to exclude cost tracking.  
- Focus team resources on functional delivery and user-facing features.  
- Revisit cost modelling only if the project expands into a production-grade system.  

## Outcome
The team decided **not to track deployment, hosting, or licensing costs** for this project, as it is a small-scale MVP and relies primarily on free or open-source tools.  
This avoids unnecessary overhead and keeps focus on delivering functionality.
