## Background
The client suggested including **onboarding questions** during the sign-up process to collect user information such as purpose and interests. These questions should not be too long, to avoid user drop-off, but should provide valuable data for personalisation and filtering.

## Relevant Data
- Client feedback emphasised onboarding as a way to collect user info.  
- Information categories include purpose, industry sector interests, and company attributes.  
- Excessive questions may discourage users from completing onboarding.  

## Options Considered
**Option 1: Skip onboarding questions**  
- Pro: Faster sign-up, no user friction.  
- Con: Misses opportunity to collect valuable data.  

**Option 2: Long, detailed questionnaire**  
- Pro: Rich dataset for personalisation.  
- Con: High user drop-off risk.  

**Option 3: Short, focused onboarding questions (Chosen)**  
- Pro: Balance between data collection and usability.  
- Con: Less detailed than a full survey.  

## Action Items
- Limit onboarding to 3–5 key questions.  
- Focus on purpose, sectors of interest, and company attributes.  
- Provide skip option to reduce friction.  
- Design onboarding UI with one question per screen and progress indicator.  

## Outcome
The team decided to **implement short onboarding questions** during sign-up, focused on purpose and interests.  
This ensures useful data collection while maintaining a smooth user experience.
